\begin{abstract}
We develop a conditional exact boundary formula for the minimum external
boundary of $R$-element subsets of arrangement graphs $A(n,k)$ for all cluster
sizes $R \ge 2$, subject to two explicit propositions (the Universal Boundary
Inequality and the Hamming Ball Evaluation) that remain open in full generality.
Under those propositions, the lexicographic Hamming Ball is the
boundary-minimizing subset for all $R \ge 2$, whose defect matches the
cumulative binary weight sequence (OEIS A000788); notably, at $R=3$ the sparse
and dense limits coincide. To facilitate this, we introduce the \emph{algebraic
defect framework}, replacing the geometric compression methods traditional to
hypercube analysis, which we show fail on arrangement graphs because vertices
must use distinct symbols. We establish a Fixed-Topology Asymptotic Penalty
result: for each fixed sub-optimal topology, the boundary gap to the Hamming
Ball grows without bound as $(n-k) \to \infty$, at rate at least $\Delta D \cdot
(n-k)$ up to a topology-dependent constant, so the Hamming Ball eventually has
smaller boundary than that fixed topology as the network scales. Finally, we
partially classify the achievable defect spectrum, proving that it forms a
``Topological Funnel'' that exhibits primary and secondary fractures near powers
of 2, while maintaining a continuous spectrum at lower defect densities. The
core graph theory is mechanized in Lean 4, conditional upon two explicit
propositions (Propositions~\ref{prop:universal} and~\ref{prop:hb-eval}), with
Proposition~\ref{prop:hb-eval} computationally verified up to $R=260$ and
Proposition~\ref{prop:universal} tested exhaustively over connected $R$-vertex
subsets for $R \le 10$, and separately over \emph{all} (including disconnected)
subsets for $R \le 6$ across 26 parameter rows of $A(n,k)$, up to
$3.24\times10^9$ subsets in a single row, with no counterexample found.
\end{abstract}
