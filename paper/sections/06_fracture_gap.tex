\section{The Fracture Gap and Defect Continuity}\label{sec:fracture}

The defect framework proves the universal upper bound
$D(V')\le \Eseq(|V'|)$, but it does not classify equality cases or the
intermediate values of $D$. Earlier drafts of this section treated defect as
the number of internal edges and transferred Boolean-hypercube statements
directly to arrangement graphs. That transfer is invalid: arrangement lines
are cliques, and a three-vertex triangle in one line has defect $2$ while its
internal edge count is $3$. The hypercube embedding therefore supplies
specific witnesses, not a classification of all arrangement-graph subsets.

The statements below are retained as explicitly labeled conjectures and finite
observations. They are not used by the conditional boundary theorem or by the
Lean capstone.

\begin{conjecture}[Proposed fracture gap]\label{thm:fracture}
For $R=2^d$, a suitable connectedness and shape-restricted class of
arrangement-graph subsets may have a defect gap below the Hamming-ball value
$\Eseq(R)=d2^{d-1}$. The unrestricted statement for all connected subsets is
open and must not be inferred from Harper's edge-isoperimetric theorem.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{equation}\label{eq:fracture-gap}
\gamma(R) =
\begin{cases}
d-1 & \text{as a conjectural Boolean-subcube value when }R=2^d,\\
0 & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
\end{equation}

\begin{conjecture}[Defect continuity]\label{thm:continuity}
For a separately specified shape class, every integer defect in the proposed
interval from the spanning-tree value $R-1$ through $\Eseq(R-1)+1$ may be
achievable. The previous pendant-leaf induction is not a proof: a suitable
leaf need not exist, and the arithmetic overlap argument has counterexamples.
\end{conjecture}

The finite computation for $R=8$ found the defect values
$\{7,8,9,10,12\}$ in the tested connected-subset search. In particular, it
found no value $11$ in that finite cell. This records evidence only; it does
not prove the unrestricted fracture or continuity claims.

\begin{conjecture}[Secondary fracture gaps]\label{thm:secondary-gap}
For $R=2^d-1$, a secondary gap below $\Eseq(R)$ may occur in a restricted
shape class. The asserted gap for every connected arrangement-graph subset
is open and is not established by the Boolean-hypercube calculation.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Partial defect classification]\label{thm:classification}
A classification of achievable defect values may consist of a low-defect
interval together with isolated dense values, but the displayed spectrum is
not proved for arbitrary connected subsets of $A(n,k)$. Any valid version
must account for clique lines, embedding parameters, and the distinction
between defect and internal edge count.
\end{conjecture}

The topological funnel discussion in Section~\ref{sec:funnel} should therefore
be read as a program of conjectures and finite diagnostics. The proven
statements used by the paper are the defect upper bound, the exact boundary
identities, and the conditional restricted-boundary composition; the fracture,
continuity, secondary-gap, and equality-classification claims remain outside
that proof chain.
