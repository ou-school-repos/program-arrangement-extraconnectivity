\section{Conclusion}

The Algebraic Defect Framework provides a conditional exact boundary formula for
the minimum external boundary of $R$-element subsets of $A(n,k)$ for all $R$,
subject to two open propositions. Replacing previously used geometric tools with
a verified algebraic framework, the approach yields fixed-topology asymptotic
Hamming-ball dominance and a partial defect-spectrum classification. Closing the
Universal Boundary Inequality (Proposition~\ref{prop:universal}) and
establishing the reduction from boundary minimization to exact
$(R{-}1)$-extraconnectivity remain the principal open problems.
