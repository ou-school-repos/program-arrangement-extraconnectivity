\section{Formal Verification in Lean 4}\label{app:lean}

The Algebraic Defect Framework and the dimensional scaling bijections are
mechanically verified in the Lean 4 proof assistant \cite{leanprover}, including
the subadditivity lemma (\texttt{E\_add\_min\_le}) and the defect bound
(\texttt{sum\_unique\_roots\_lower\_bound}) underlying Theorem~\ref{thm:defect}.
The boundary decomposition~\eqref{eq:boundary} and the exact comparative
identities used in Theorem~\ref{thm:penalty} are likewise mechanized in
\texttt{Arrangement/PenaltyExact.lean} via \texttt{boundary\_identity},
\texttt{penalty\_exact}, \texttt{penalty\_defect}, and \texttt{penalty\_ge}; the
asymptotic-dominance conclusion in the prose is then the immediate large-$(n-k)$
reading of those exact formulas. The Fracture Gap, Defect Continuity, Secondary
Fracture Gap, and Partial Defect Classification results of
Section~\ref{sec:fracture} are proven on paper from Harper's edge-isoperimetric
theorem but are not yet mechanized. The entire graph theory of $A(n,k)$ is
formalized from first principles; rather than declaring the unproved bounds as
global axioms, we express the remaining capstone dependency by parameterizing
the final exact boundary-minimum theorem with the explicit
\texttt{RestrictedLowerBound} hypothesis --- a statement about
\texttt{external\_neighbors} under the hypercube embedding conditions, i.e.
Theorem~\ref{thm:main}(i)--(ii), not about
the graph's extraconnectivity metric itself (Section~\ref{sec:defect}'s
remark and Section~\ref{sec:funnel} discuss why that further reduction is
independently open). The direct proof supplies
\texttt{HBCrossCollisions}. This cleanly isolates the classical extremal set
theory from the novel algebraic machinery
while ensuring that the compiled theorems are completely transparent, verified,
and free of raw, uninstantiated axiom commands.

The capstone theorem conditionally combines the supplied restricted lower bound
with the internally verified Hamming-ball construction. \emph{Note:} In the Lean source, the code
variables \texttt{E\_seq} and \texttt{C\_constant} correspond to $\Eseq(R)$ and
$\Cconst(R)$ respectively.

{\small
\begin{lstlisting}[language={}, breaklines=true]
theorem arrangement_boundary_minimum (R n k : Nat) (h_cond : can_embed_hypercube R n k)
    (h_lower : forall (R n k : Nat), RestrictedLowerBound R n k) :
  (exists V' : Finset (ArrVertex n k), V'.card = R /\
    external_neighbors V' = (R * k - E_seq R) * (n - k) - C_constant R) /\
  (forall V' : Finset (ArrVertex n k), V'.card = R ->
    external_neighbors V' >= (R * k - E_seq R) * (n - k) - C_constant R)
\end{lstlisting}
}
